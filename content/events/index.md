The CMS Open Source Program Office participates in conferences and events across open source, healthcare, and civic technology to share our work and learn from the community. Our presentations explore topics such as building an open source culture in federal agencies, improving upon repository best practices, and collaborating on software that supports public services.

Explore our presentations, news coverage, interviews, and event highlights below:
