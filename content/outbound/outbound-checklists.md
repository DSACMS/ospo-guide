Our outbound review checklists outlines our process of approving CMS-developed software to be released as open source:

_Benefits & Risks_

- [Evaluate cost savings, extensibility, innovation, and other development benefits](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#state-the-benefits-of-open-sourcing-the-project)
- [Evaluate the potential security, financial, and privacy risks](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#state-the-risks-of-open-sourcing-the-project)

_Code Review & Analysis_

- [Ensure Code Quality](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#code-review)
- [Detect PII/PHI](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#code-analysis)
- [Detect Secrets and Keys](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#review-commit-history)
- [Detect Security Vulnerabilities](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#code-analysis)
- [Identify Licensing and Provenance](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#review-licensing)

_Metadata & Hygiene_

- [Ensure repository files exist and are complete](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#review-repository-hygiene)
- [Ensure repository metadata exist in a code.json file and are complete](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#review-project-metadata)

_Sign-off_

-[Acknowledgment and/or Approvals from Technical, Business, and Security Stakeholders](https://github.com/DSACMS/repo-scaffolder/blob/main/tier3/checklist.md#sign-off-on-risk-acceptance-of-open-sourcing-the-software-product)
