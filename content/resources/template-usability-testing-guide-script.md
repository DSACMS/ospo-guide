# **Template**: Usability Testing Interview Script

Use this template as an outline to create your interview script for your project. This script contains both questions and tasks/activities for the participant; you should adjust, edit, and omit content for your project’s needs. 

Note: As mentioned in the checklist and on the research plan instructions, sometimes it is beneficial to collect a signed consent form from the research participant; other times, a verbal recognition captured on a recording is sufficient (what we have depicted below). 

---

Hi, this is **\[INTERVIEWER NAME\]** from **\[ORGANIZATION NAME\]**. I’m here today with **\[NOTETAKER NAME\]**, who will be taking notes from our call. Thank you very much for volunteering your time to help us **\[INSERT PURPOSE AND GOALS OF RESEARCH\]**. 

Before we get started, I want to go over a few reminders and ask for your permission to record our session today so we can make accurate observations and notes. 

*\[Confirm\]* 

* You are not required to participate and may opt out at any time. Your participation in this session in no way affects **\[YOUR ORGANIZATION’S\]** access or future access to **\[repository, etc.\]**.  
* There are no “right” or “wrong” answers or actions during the activities portion of the session.  
* Can you confirm you are able to connect to the internet, open links, and share your screen?
