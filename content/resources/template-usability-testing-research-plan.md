# **Success Criteria**

*What does success look like for this research?* 

It might be helpful to lay out success criteria for the testing session, including criteria for post-test survey responses if collected: 

* Task completion rate  \> 80% (not including confidence score)  
* [System Usability Score (SUS)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/measuring-perceived-usability/) \> 68  (to be considered above average in usability). A SUS score of 80.3 is “A” report  
* Confidence score (\<=4)  
* No critical issues preventing core tasks  
* Users can confidently determine **\[the offering\]** that best suits their needs.
